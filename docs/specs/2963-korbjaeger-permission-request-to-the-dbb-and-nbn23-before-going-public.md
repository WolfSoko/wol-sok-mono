# Spec: Korbjäger — permission request to the DBB (and NBN23) before going public

Issue: [#2963](https://github.com/WolfSoko/wol-sok-mono/issues/2963). Part of [#2861](https://github.com/WolfSoko/wol-sok-mono/issues/2861); builds on [ADR 0018](../adr/0018-korbjaeger-dbb-data-behind-a-caching-proxy.md) (and its amendment of [#2988](https://github.com/WolfSoko/wol-sok-mono/issues/2988)), [ADR 0019](../adr/0019-korbjaeger-privacy-stance.md), spike [#2947](https://github.com/WolfSoko/wol-sok-mono/issues/2947) ([spec](2947-spike-korbjaeger-does-the-dbb-offer-a-live-data-source-and-would-it-agree-to-its-use.md)) and spike [#2977](https://github.com/WolfSoko/wol-sok-mono/issues/2977) ([spec](2977-spike-korbjaeger-dbb-scores-terms-of-use-and-an-official-nbn23-partner-api-access-no-scraping.md)).

No code changes. The deliverable is two finished letters the owner sends themselves, plus a note on what each possible answer changes in the app.

## Goal

The owner can send the DBB and NBN23 a request that names exactly what Korbjäger reads, how often, what it keeps and what it asks for, so that the answers settle whether the app may go public, whether live data or a DBB.Scores link is ever possible, and at what request rate.

## Non-goals

- Sending the letters: the owner does that, from their own address.
- Any change to the app, the proxy, the club index or their cache times. What the answers change is a follow-up issue once they arrive.
- Legal advice. The letters ask; they claim no right.

| 2   | Live data: may it be used, from whom (DBB or NBN23), at what rate; a shared cache of 15 to 30 s means about 2 (at 30 s) to 4 (at 15 s) requests a minute per live game and CDN edge.                                                                       | DBB, NBN23 |

## Decisions

| Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Two letters**: one to the DBB (`info@basketball-bund.de`, copying its data protection officer `datenschutz@basketball-bund.de`), one to NBN23 (`info.de@nbn23.tech`, copying the DBB).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | The DBB owns the data, the digital scoresheet and the players' consent; NBN23 owns DBB.Scores and its live feed. #2977 found NBN23's partnerships run through the organiser, so the DBB sees the NBN23 letter too.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| The letters are in **German**, kept beside this spec as `docs/specs/2963-letter-dbb.de.md` and `docs/specs/2963-letter-nbn23.de.md`; everything around them (this spec, the plan) is English.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Both recipients answer German mail (NBN23's `info.de@` is its German desk); a letter is text the recipient reads, like the app's German UI (CLAUDE.md rule 10).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Ask for the deep link before data** in the NBN23 letter.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | #2977: a link costs NBN23 nothing and leaves its Gold sale intact, so it is the likelier yes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| The DBB letter says **openly that Korbjäger already reads the API** today, unlisted (`noindex`, no sitemap, not linked from the portfolio), for a small circle of parents, and offers to stop or change it at once.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | ADR 0018 had the app run as "a guest the DBB barely notices" until asked; asking now without saying so would hide what the DBB can see in its logs anyway (the User-Agent names the app).                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **No scraping instead of asking** (the owner's comment of the issue). The letters go out; the app stays unlisted until the answers are in.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | basketball-bund.net is a single-page app that reads the same `/rest` API, so scraping its pages is the same reads with more load. Korbjäger serves other parents, not only its owner, so "private use" does not cover it; the DBB's Impressum reserves every use outside copyright's limits to written consent, and NBN23's app terms forbid crawlers and bots without the owner's consent (#2977). |
| The figures in the letters come from the code as of this spec: the eight reads (`dbbHttpRequest`, ADR 0018), the cache times `PROXY_CACHE_SEKUNDEN` (60 s browser, 300 s CDN, 3600 s for a finished Spielbericht), `CLUB_INDEX_CACHE_SECONDS` (a day in the CDN), the User-Agents `Korbjaeger/<version> (+https://korb.wolsok.de)` and `Korbjaeger-ClubIndex (+https://korb.wolsok.de)`, the crawl's pace `CLUB_INDEX_DBB` (one request at a time, a second apart) and its schedule (weekly per Verband, about 2,600 requests a week). | So the DBB can match what it sees in its logs. No spec ties the letters to the constants: they are sent once; a later change is told to the DBB in a reply, not by editing the letter.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| The sender's name, address and signature are placeholders (`[…]`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | The owner sends it; the repo holds no signature. Their details stand in the Impressum (`SITE_OWNER`) anyway.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| A section **"What each answer changes"** in this spec names the code each answer touches.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | So the follow-up issue the answers start is quick to write.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

## What each answer changes

| Answer                                                    | Change in the app                                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| DBB agrees to the reads (item 1)                          | ADR 0018 records it; the privacy policy may name the DBB's consent; going public (portfolio entry, still `noindex` per ADR 0019 until decided separately) becomes possible. |
| DBB names a request rate or cache time                    | `PROXY_CACHE_SEKUNDEN` (`apps/korbjaeger/src/shared/proxy-cache.ts`), `CLUB_INDEX_DBB.pauseMs` and `CLUB_INDEX_SCHEDULE` (`apps/korbjaeger-functions/src/config.ts`).       |
| DBB refuses                                               | The app goes offline (ADR 0018: nothing else depends on the API); `CLUB_INDEX_ON` takes the crawl out of the next deploy.                                                   |
| DBB refuses the crawl but offers a club search            | A ninth read behind `/api/clubs`, the crawl switched off (ADR 0018 amendment).                                                                                              |
| DBB says its consent does not cover Kader or Spielerwerte | The Spiel page's Kader and Spielerwerte (`src/app/spiel-seite/`) and the Kind marking (`src/app/kind/`) are reconsidered; ADR 0019 amended.                                 |
| NBN23 permits the deep link and names a format            | A link on the Spiel page (`SpielSeite`), its target in the lib; privacy policy names DBB.Scores as a link target.                                                           |
| NBN23 offers a licence                                    | A live port or second adapter (#2947), a live route with `s-maxage` 15 to 30 s, privacy policy and ADR 0019 updated. Never before both agreed in writing.                   |

## Acceptance

1. `2963-letter-dbb.de.md` and `2963-letter-nbn23.de.md` together ask every item of the table above, each item in the letter its row names.
2. Every figure in the letters matches the code and ADRs named under Decisions (checked in review).
3. The DBB letter says the app already reads the API, unlisted, and offers to stop or adjust.
4. Neither letter names a person: only the recipients' functional addresses and placeholders for the sender.
5. Nothing outside `docs/` changes; `nx format:check` passes.
