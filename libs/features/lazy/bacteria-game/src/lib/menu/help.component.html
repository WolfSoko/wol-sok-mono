1. 1

    Steer the crosshair

    Your colony follows its crosshair. Well fed bacteria divide, and the drops of jam supercharge whoever grabs them first.

  2. 2

    Fire the boost

    For a few seconds your bacteria are faster and more aggressive, then the boost recharges - fire it to break a frontline.

  3. 3

    Eat the other colony

    Surround the enemy to eat it. The match ends when one colony is gone, or when the clock of a timed level runs out, or when a colony has eaten enough pellets on a harvest level - win fast and clean for a better medallion.

    Grey colonies are neutral: nobody steers them and they count for no side. Eat them first and your army grows.

Touch

Push the joystick zone at your edge and tap the boost button - or drag your crosshair across the arena.
